## What this course is not

- not a features checklist: brush size, colour, undo, export
- not "learn ten techniques and combine them"
- not software that makes a picture *for* you

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Instrument, not artwork

An artwork is a fixed thing you show someone.

An instrument is something you hand to someone --- and it behaves differently
every time, because a different hand is now involved.

---

## Everything this course marks is an instrument

- First Instrument (week 6): one build, one constraint, handed to a stranger
- Exhibition Piece (week 12): running unattended, for anyone to walk up to
- Critique Practice: how you used every studio in between

---

## The shape of a studio

1. short demo of last week's build
2. feedback aimed at your least-certain decision
3. one new constraint or capability for next week

Repeats for eleven more weeks.

---

## Before next week

Nothing more than:

- a canvas
- a line that follows the pointer

No brush width, no colour, no meaning yet. Just the one continuous gesture
everything else complicates.
